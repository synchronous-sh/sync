The problem

You save hundreds of things. You never see them again.

Native save buttons are a graveyard. TikToks, Reels, tweets, Reddit threads, articles, and screenshots pile up across apps. When you actually need an idea, a recipe, a tool, or a quote, you cannot find it.

Bookmarks keep the URL. They do not keep the meaning. synchronous is built for the moment you think: I know I saved this somewhere.

Capture

See it. Share it. Done.

  1. 01

    Share to sync

    From any app, open the iOS share sheet and tap sync. No folders. No tags. No waiting.

  2. 02

    Saved in about a second

    The original link, screenshot, or note is stored immediately. Understanding happens in the background.

  3. 03

    Find it when you need it

    Search by vague memory, browse collections that fill themselves in, or ask across everything you’ve saved.

The library

Not another chatbot. Memory you can search.

Hybrid search

Exact keywords, meaning, people, and recency. “That video about sleep from months ago” should still work.

Collections that appear

Topics like recipes, travel, agents, or design cluster on their own. You can still pin, rename, or edit.

Related across platforms

A TikTok, an article, and a screenshot from three months apart can sit next to each other if they belong together.

Ask your saves

Ask a question when you want to. Answers cite the original saves. The chat is not the product.

Resurfacing

Not “remember this?” More like: you’ve been saving this topic again — here is what you found four months ago.

Learn and news, later

Home, Videos, Learn, and News sit on top of the same library so what you consume can become what you know.

The magic moment is not another summary. It is “I forgot I saved this.”

Privacy

Your library belongs to you.

Saves are private by default. There is no social feed at launch. Export or delete your library, delete a save, or delete your account.

We do not train models on your private library without explicit permission. The original source always stays one tap away.

How we build

  • Saving takes less than two seconds.
  • You should not have to organize by hand.
  • A save stays useful even if enrichment fails.
  • Search works on fuzzy memory, not just exact titles.
  • Capture first. Understanding second.

Questions

Before you join

What platforms work at launch?
TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, Spotify, websites, screenshots, copied text, and notes — via the iOS share sheet.
Will it import my entire TikTok or Instagram saved folder?
No. Platform APIs do not give reliable access to those folders. Share what you want to keep. That is the habit that actually works.
Is this only for social video?
Social is the wedge. The product is a home for everything you want to remember from the internet.
When does iOS land?
We are building the native app first. Waitlist members get early access as we open TestFlight.
